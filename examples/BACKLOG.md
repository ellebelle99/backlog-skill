## Details (optional — only for items needing more than a title)

**#3 — Passport renewal.** Expires in spring, and renewals can take several weeks. Needs a recent photo, the old passport and the fee. Book the appointment first, since slots fill up before holiday season.

**#5 — Home insurance quotes.** Renewal date is on the policy letter. Get three quotes using the same cover and excess so they compare. Ask the current insurer for a loyalty price before switching.

**#8 — Summer holiday.** Fix the dates with the others first, then book flights. Check passport validity (see #3) and whether the destination needs a visa or insurance.

**#6 — Dripping tap.** Probably a worn washer or cartridge. Note the tap brand, turn off the water supply before opening it, and call a plumber if it still drips afterwards.

Notice that #8 points back to #3. Details can reference other items, which is useful when one task blocks another.

I left out the quick items such as the dentist booking and the gym cancellation. A note would add nothing, and the skill's rule is that details are for items that need more than a title.
